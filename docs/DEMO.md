# DOMINO demo · From profile to company introduction

Aim for **1:45** in a two-minute slot. Leave 15 seconds for clicks and transitions.

## Before recording

- Prepare Rahul's demo LinkedIn URL and an optional résumé or portfolio link. Use example profiles for the other candidates and label sample market data on screen.
- Open Rahul's onboarding, Nova Labs' recruiter dashboard, and Nova's company dashboard in three ready-to-switch views. Start with Rahul's intro absent from the recruiter inbox. Preload the other candidate suggestions.
- Use Nova Labs as the company Rahul selects. Keep its name and the relevant role visible through the handoff. Prepare a dated comparison with Atlas and one other relevant peer.

## Run of show

### 0:00–0:15 · Rahul onboards

**Show** Rahul entering his name and LinkedIn URL. The page also offers an optional résumé, portfolio, or work sample. Submit and go straight to the recommendations.

**Say** “Meet Rahul. He gives DOMINO his name and LinkedIn profile. He can add anything else that shows his work.”

### 0:15–0:35 · Rahul sees who DOMINO can introduce him to

**Show** A short list of company introductions. Each card shows the company, the opportunity, and one reason it fits Rahul. Expand Nova Labs to show a relevant line from the opening beside evidence from Rahul's profile or work.

**Say** “These are companies DOMINO can introduce Rahul to. Nova is hiring for inference serving, and Rahul has worked on running models in production. He can see why Nova came up.”

### 0:35–0:50 · The introduction reaches Nova

**Show** Rahul selects Nova and reviews the short intro, the recipient, and the material he is about to share. He confirms. Show the status the system can verify, then switch to Nova's recruiter dashboard.

**Say** “Rahul chooses Nova and reviews what the recruiter will receive. Let's see where it lands.”

### 0:50–1:13 · The recruiter opens the intro

**Show** Rahul arrives as a **New intro** in the recruiter dashboard. The same dashboard shows other candidates DOMINO has surfaced under **Suggested**. Open Rahul's row so the recruiter can see the role, the work he shared, and his request. Hold on this view for a beat.

**Say** “That's Rahul's intro arriving at Nova. The recruiter can review his request alongside people DOMINO has suggested for the team. Rahul's row shows that he already chose to reach out.”

### 1:13–1:38 · Nova opens the company dashboard

**Show** Start on the full company overview with hiring, headcount, funding, talent movement, and competitors. Open **Competitor Analysis** and compare Nova, Atlas, and a third peer over the same hiring window. Highlight one difference and open the dated source behind it. For example, Nova has opened roles in inference serving and GPU kernels while Atlas's visible openings focus on serving.

**Say** “Rahul saw one opening. Nova can see that opening alongside the capabilities its peers are hiring for, how their teams are changing, and the sources behind the comparison.”

### 1:38–1:45 · Close

**Show** End on the company dashboard with Nova still selected and the comparison visible.

**Say** “Rahul got his intro. Nova got an interested candidate and can see who else is hiring for the same skills.”

## Recording checks

- Preview only introductions with a real route in the demo environment. Use a test company account for delivery and confirm the intro reaches its inbox once. Keep submission and receipt statuses distinct in the recording.
- Keep the difference between an incoming intro and a suggested candidate visible in the recruiter view. Suggested candidates' sharing settings should permit the details shown.
- Check the peer comparison against the data loaded for the run. Label the period and source coverage. State what the postings show and keep any interpretation about company strategy conditional.
- Rehearse the candidate-to-recruiter switch until the audience can follow it without an explanation. Time the whole cut at 1:45.
